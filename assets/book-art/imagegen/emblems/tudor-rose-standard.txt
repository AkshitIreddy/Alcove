Prompt 2 — accepted generation

Create a clean transparent PNG emblem, like a one-colour vector cutout.

Name and identity: Tudor rose standard. Draw a clearly recognizable heraldic Tudor rose with exactly five large rounded outer petals, exactly five smaller inner petals, and one round centre. Mount it on a short straight stem with two small symmetrical curled leaves at the base. The flower should look formal and heraldic, not naturalistic.

Artwork: every visible mark must be the exact same solid dark sepia #432934. Use broad solid shapes and clean cut-out gaps between petals. Strictly flat. Crisp antialiased contours. Strong silhouette that still reads at 24 pixels. Centre it vertically with generous clear space around it.

Transparency: the entire surrounding canvas must be genuine alpha transparency. Produce no black background, no coloured background, no checkerboard pattern, no halo, no vignette, and no matte.

Forbidden: gradients, tonal changes, highlights, shadows, lighting, glow, haze, blur, texture, paper, leather, metal effect, gold, border, circle, badge, medallion, plate, shield, crown, words, letters, numbers, watermark, realistic flower shading, extra petals, thorns, branch.
