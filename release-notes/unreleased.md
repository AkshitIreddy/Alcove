## More reliable picture requests

The Agent now handles more variations in Cohere’s streamed replies instead of
stopping with an unusable-response error. Image requests remain tied to the
exact attachment and must still pass Alcove’s preview checks before insertion.

## Smoother notebook editing

The six-dot block handles stay beside their blocks when panels open or the book
changes size, instead of briefly jumping into the middle of the page. Release
notes are also shorter and focused on what readers will notice.
