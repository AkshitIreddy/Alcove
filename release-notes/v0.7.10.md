## Freer canvas controls

Focus mode and the image workspace now zoom with the wheel and pan while the
middle mouse button is held, even over page content. Focus writing can cross
the paper edge without selecting text, its tool pocket can close, and image
markup stays stable while drawing. Fullscreen images fit every viewport edge
and the recovery control is simply named Center.

## Smoother pages and a quieter Studio

Page turns keep their complete raster frame visible until newly landed images
are decoded, preventing the post-turn flash. Book Studio moves Previous Look to
a small preview-corner arrow and removes redundant custom-colour swatch rows.

## Better-bound books

The previous title catalogue has been replaced by 25 material-specific title
constructions, from vellum slips and morocco fillets to Grolier, Arts and
Crafts, armorial and lobed fields. Six new curated cover frames bring the frame
catalogue to eighteen.
