## Page-aware pictures and diagrams

Pictures can still grow into the paper's quiet margins, but an extreme resize
no longer sends the picture itself to the next page. A new fit action uses only
the available space around the image; blank-cell clicks dismiss its controls,
and its alignment icon now follows the selected alignment.

Tall flowcharts split at safe gaps between node layers and continue across as
many fixed pages as needed. Every fragment retains one editable source, edits
rebuild the complete chart, and moving it back to a previous page re-splits it
instead of producing a cannot-fit error.

## Clear feedback and real navigation

Long Notebook Script insertions now remain behind an opaque Read, Prepare,
Place and Check progress card rather than flashing white while pages settle.
Large wrapped headings have tighter line rhythm without the accidental gap
above them. The thumbnail filmstrip now uses lightweight renders of each real
page—including images, diagrams, cards and paper styles—instead of invented
preview bars.
