## Writing that meets the paper

Text on lined pages now sits on the printed rules consistently. Large headings
occupy a deliberate opening band, paragraphs after them return to the paper
rhythm, and bullet lists no longer drift higher or lower with each item. The
correction follows the small, default and large reading settings.

Transparent two-column layouts now align their internal writing to the same
page grid. Margin notes, map pins and wax-seal notes instead receive a clean
paper surface of their own, so the page's rules cannot cut through text with
independent spacing. Inline editor sentinels are also kept out of picture
styling, preserving heading and sticker line geometry.
