## Timelines keep every colour

Timeline cards now honour the complete stationer's tint catalogue. Colours
such as forest and coral stay visible instead of quietly falling back to plain
paper, and the same correction covers every tint offered by Alcove's colour
picker.

Entries without an explicit colour now receive a restrained rotating accent,
while a colour written in Notebook Script always wins. Mixed timelines remain
easy to scan without requiring a colour tail on every line.
