## Working checklists

To-do checkboxes are visible and clickable again. You can check items with a mouse or keyboard, and their checked state stays saved when you reopen the book.

## More harmonious book colours

Surprise me now coordinates its generated colours around any book colour you hold. Spines and covers keep their own tonal variation without accidentally pairing unrelated colour families. Your manually chosen colours and deliberately held contrasting colours stay intact.
