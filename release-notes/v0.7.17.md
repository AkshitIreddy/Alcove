## Dropdowns that continue across pages

Expanded dropdowns now use the space remaining on a page and continue onto the next, instead of jumping there as a whole or cutting off long content at the bottom. The heading repeats on the continuation so it is easy to follow.

Long paragraphs and nested lists can continue across the page boundary while preserving their text, formatting and list numbering. The continuation stays editable and can be collapsed or expanded. Typing across the boundary carries the caret with the text and remains undoable.
