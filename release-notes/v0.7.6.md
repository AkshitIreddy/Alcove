## More reliable picture requests

The Agent now carries attached images through the whole authoring flow instead of
dropping them before review. It can recover from noisy Cohere stream replies and
still keep source grounding intact, so image-led requests produce the expected
notes and the expected infographic page instead of going blank or drifting.

## Smoother notebook editing

You can ask a follow-up question while a draft review is pending without
discarding prior AI work. The preview remains visible and review stays actionable,
while settings and workspace behavior in notebook panels were tightened to avoid
accidental zooming and dragging in places where it previously felt random.
