## Smoother notebook editing

The block menu now includes a normal Paste action, and any block can move to
the previous page when there is room. Rail panels keep their complete outline,
including both lower corners, while long catalogues and pickers still scroll
inside the sheet.

## A more decisive Agent

After you answer a clarification, the Agent continues the task instead of
starting another round of questions. This release also strengthens the
real-browser checks around pending previews, image follow-ups and notebook
editing, and replaces the oversized reader manual with a shorter guide that
puts downloads first.
