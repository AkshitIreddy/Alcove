## A steadier Agent

Image-assisted notebook tasks now recover when Cohere returns a malformed or
incomplete draft response. Alcove requests one corrected complete draft, keeps
normal rate-limit and network retries, and never stores truncated output.
Brief picture requests stay grounded in the exact attachment and keep their
reviewed preview available while you ask a follow-up question.

## A fuller reader guide

The README once again leads with customizable shelves, books, pages,
wallpaper, writing desks, sound and local ownership, followed by the complete
illustrated feature tour and a compact download section. The AI helper remains
an optional feature rather than the centre of Alcove.
