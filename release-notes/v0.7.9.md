## A freer reading desk

Focus mode now centres a single page and lets you pan and zoom across an open
canvas. Mouse writing has pens, colours, sizes, undo and redo, with explicit
save or discard. The image viewer gains the same free movement, fullscreen
workspace and durable image annotations.

## A clearer notebook

The table of contents lists real headings, highlights one active destination
and can collapse to major sections. Context menus are configurable and use
Alcove scrollbars, books reopen where you left them, page turns wait for their
art, and Book Studio can restore earlier generated looks.

## Better pictures and bindings

Multi-image Agent requests preserve every attachment in order instead of
silently dropping later pictures. Title plates and emblems have been rebuilt
around a smaller, carefully vetted set of book-like designs.
